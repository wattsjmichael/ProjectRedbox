## Short Description

A dark sci-fi action roguelite built around deliberate weapon combos, rare loot, and persistent Hunter progression.

## Long Description

Project Redbox sends Hunters into hostile industrial ruins to clear escalating encounters, recover randomized equipment, develop an autonomous Core, and confront the Wyrm. Five weapons reward different positioning and combo rhythms, while rare Red Boxes turn every fight into a potential gear decision. Between drops, locally saved Hunter profiles preserve equipment, Core growth, and progression.

## Key Features

- Five distinct three-step weapons and configuration-driven finishers.
- Randomized weapon stats, prefixes, suffixes, and visible rare Red Boxes.
- Structured runs through the gated Wastes biome and a dedicated boss arena.
- Persistent local Hunter profiles, inventory, armor foundation, and Core development.
- Crisp top-down retro-console science-fiction direction with current temporary art.
