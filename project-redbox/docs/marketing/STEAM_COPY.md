## Recommended Default — Loot-First

### One-Line Pitch

A dark sci-fi action roguelite where deliberate weapon combos turn every rare drop into a new way to hunt.

### Short Description

Fight through hostile industrial ruins, master five distinct weapons, recover randomized gear, and return stronger. Project Redbox combines fast top-down runs with rare-loot excitement and persistent Hunter progression.

### Long Description

Enter The Wastes as a Hunter searching for equipment worth bringing home. Clear locked combat zones, break enemy formations with deliberate three-step weapon combos, and push toward the Wyrm waiting below.

Every weapon changes how you position. Line up Piercing Rounds with the Rifle, clear close quarters with the Scattergun, detonate Cannon finishers, chain Photon energy through clustered enemies, or master the Greatsword’s timing windows. Random stats and affixes make each useful find a decision—not just a larger number.

Between drops, return to the Hunter Bay. Equip salvage, feed unwanted gear to your evolving Core, select the next challenge, and continue an independently saved Hunter profile.

Project Redbox is inspired by classic online action-RPG loot hunts, retro top-down action, and modern run-based combat. It is currently in development; visuals and features shown may change.

### Feature Bullets

- Five weapons with distinct combo rhythms and finishers.
- Randomized equipment, identity-focused affixes, and unmistakable Red Boxes.
- Gated combat zones, mixed enemy roles, and the Wyrm boss.
- Persistent Hunter profiles, inventory, armor, levels, and Core growth.
- Dark industrial science fiction rendered for immediate top-down readability.
