# Social Post Templates

**Last Updated:** 2026-08-03  
**Status:** Reusable structures; replace brackets with real footage and facts

Use a human developer voice, one clear point per post, and few or no hashtags. Rewrite opening lines for each platform rather than copying identical text everywhere.

## New Weapon

- **Hook:** “[Weapon] changes where you want to stand.”
- **Main text:** Explain its three-hit rhythm, finisher, and one tradeoff.
- **CTA:** Ask which positioning choice viewers would make or link the build.
- **Visual:** Full combo against the enemy type it counters.
- **Platform note:** Short clip for X/Bluesky/TikTok; mechanics paragraph for itch/site.

## Rare Drop

- **Hook:** “That red glow still stops me mid-fight.”
- **Main text:** Show drop, reveal, and the item decision; include the real affix name.
- **CTA:** “Would you equip it or feed it to the Core?”
- **Visual:** Uncut Red Box sequence.

## Boss Reveal

- **Hook:** “The Wyrm is waiting past the last gate.”
- **Main text:** Describe the combat question, not lore that is not finalized.
- **CTA:** Link devlog/wishlist.
- **Visual:** Telegraph, dodge, and finisher impact without spoiling the entire fight.

## Core / Companion Update

- **Hook:** “Unwanted gear still has a purpose.”
- **Main text:** Show feeding result, stat gain, or evolution milestone using the current **Core** name.
- **CTA:** Ask what equipment viewers would sacrifice.
- **Visual:** Bay feed UI plus follower shot.

## Devlog Announcement

- **Hook:** “[Milestone] is about [player-facing outcome].”
- **Main text:** Three changes and why they matter.
- **CTA:** Read/play via one link.
- **Visual:** Best before/after or montage.

## Browser Demo Update

- **Hook:** “Project Redbox is playable in your browser.” *(Use only when true.)*
- **Main text:** State build scope, input, save behavior, and known limitations.
- **CTA:** Play and send focused feedback.
- **Visual:** Start-to-action clip plus visible URL/end card.

## Steam Page Announcement

- **Hook:** “Project Redbox has a Steam page.” *(Use only after publication.)*
- **Main text:** One-sentence fantasy and what following/wishlisting supports.
- **CTA:** Wishlist on Steam.
- **Visual:** Trailer cut or strongest capsule plus gameplay.

## Bug Post

- **Hook:** “[Unexpected bug in plain language].”
- **Main text:** What caused it and the focused fix; avoid embarrassing player data.
- **CTA:** Invite similar reports or link the fixed build.
- **Visual:** Short before/fix comparison.

## Before and After

- **Hook:** “Same encounter, clearer fight.”
- **Main text:** Name the readability problem and one design rule used.
- **CTA:** Ask which version communicates danger faster.
- **Visual:** Synchronized split screen.

## Development Question

- **Hook:** Ask one bounded choice: timing, readability, or preference.
- **Main text:** Give enough context and two honest tradeoffs.
- **CTA:** Request a specific answer, not generic feedback.
- **Visual:** Two labeled clips/screens.

## Milestone Celebration

- **Hook:** “[Concrete milestone] is working.”
- **Main text:** Thank players, state what is genuinely complete, and name the next priority.
- **CTA:** Play, read, or wishlist—choose one.
- **Visual:** Results screen or compact feature montage.
