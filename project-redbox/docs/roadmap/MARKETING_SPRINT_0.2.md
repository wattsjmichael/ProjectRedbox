# Marketing Sprint 0.2

**Last Updated:** 2026-08-03  
**Primary Goal:** Make it easy for someone to discover Project Redbox, understand it, play it, and wishlist it.

## Website

- Build a Redbox-focused homepage with a clear gameplay hook above the fold.
- Add **Play in Browser** and **Wishlist on Steam** as primary actions when their destinations are live.
- Include a short trailer, curated screenshots, concise feature summary, devlog archive, and itch.io/Steam links.
- Show real gameplay. Label pre-alpha footage and temporary art honestly.

## Devlogs

Mirror itch.io devlogs on DadbodStudios.com using [`../marketing/DEVLOG_TEMPLATE.md`](../marketing/DEVLOG_TEMPLATE.md). Each post should cover what changed, why it matters to players, screenshots/clips, what comes next, and one play-or-wishlist call to action.

## Sustainable Content Plan

- Publish one meaningful devlog per development milestone.
- Post two or three short gameplay updates per week when there is worthwhile material; do not manufacture daily noise.
- Capture clean clips during normal development and catalog them by feature/version.
- Reuse clips across platforms, but rewrite the opening line and call to action for each audience.

Useful subjects include a rare Red Box, Core feeding/evolution, weapon pickups, the Wyrm, Greatsword timing, loot reveals, visual before/after comparisons, funny bugs, enemy behaviors, run results, and onboarding improvements.

## Content Workflow

1. Capture a 5–20 second readable moment at 1280×720.
2. State the player-facing change in one sentence.
3. Explain one design choice or problem solved.
4. Link to the browser build, devlog, or Steam page—one main action per post.
5. Archive the source clip and published copy for later trailers.

## Success Metrics

Track whether the website and browser build are live, Steam page publication, mirrored devlogs, wishlists, browser plays, itch.io reads, trailer views, and email/community signups if those channels are added. Treat metrics as signals for iteration, not guaranteed targets.
